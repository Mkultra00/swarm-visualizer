How it works — the 60-second guide

Narrator voice: mixed TTS generated at build time — Gemini gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts where its quota allowed (tour scene), macOS say elsewhere after rate limits. No model calls happen in the browser.

  1. 1
    The idea. Think of an AI security test as a team of robot scouts exploring a practice obstacle course. What you watch here is the game tape, replayed after a closed-lab run — zero live payloads, no real attacks in the browser. Every glowing dot is one worker agent moving through five color-coded checkpoints.
  2. 2
    Read the colors, left to right. HUMAN (blue) is the starting instruction, RECON (cyan) maps the front doors, SCOPE (amber) checks what is inside the rules, PROVEN-GATE (red) is the strictest referee — hunches get stopped and verified — and PUBLISH (green) is where only verified findings score.
  3. 3
    Drive it yourself. Pick any sanitized lab run (crAPI, Juice Shop, Vault Notes) from the Sample dropdown, press Play with Narration to hear the numbers, read the plain-English Brief on the right to see which stage worked hardest, or drop your own timeline JSON — the strict schema gate blocks anything unsafe instantly.

Dots close together just mean two agents worked at the same time — not that they teamed up. Layer plot is not cause-and-effect. Attack engines stay on the offline lab machine; this site replays results only.
